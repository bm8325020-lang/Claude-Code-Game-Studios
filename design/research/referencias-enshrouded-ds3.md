## Dark Souls 3 — opening hours

| Element | How it works there | Adaptation for Seeds of the Dragon | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tutorial boss at the start (Iudex Gundyr) | First area is a cemetery that ends in a boss that teaches dodge and punish; phase 2 turns into a monster | **Fallen Guardian** in the island graveyard: phase 1 armoured knight, phase 2 a Seed corrupts him into a beast. Teaches roll, lock-on and flask | MVP |
| Hub with bonfire (Firelink Shrine) | Safe place with NPCs: level up, blacksmith, shop | **Seed Sanctuary**: central bonfire, a keeper who spends Embers on levels, a blacksmith for the sword | MVP |
| Bonfires as checkpoints | Rest, refill flasks, enemies respawn, fast travel between lit bonfires | Already in the prototype; add **lighting new bonfires** and travel between them | MVP |
| Estus Flask + upgrades | Few heals, refilled at bonfires; shards add charges | **Sap Flask**; **Sap Shards** hidden in the map add charges | MVP |
| Souls lost on death, recovered at the bloodstain | Die again before recovering and they are gone | Already in the prototype (Embers) | Done |
| Shortcuts that open back to the bonfire | Locked doors/elevators opened from the far side | Gates and ladders that open to the bonfire: the map loops back | MVP |
| Messages and bloodstains from other players | Online hints | Offline version: **inscriptions on the ground** written by the designer (hints, lore) | Later |
| Weapon Arts + FP (mana) | Special move per weapon, costs FP | **Seed skills cost Essence**, a blue bar refilled at the bonfire | MVP |
| Poise / stagger | Heavy hits break the enemy's guard | Guard bar on large enemies: fill it and they kneel for a **critical strike** | Later |
| Backstab and parry | Precise timing gives a critical | Shield **parry** (block at the right moment) | Later |
| Mimic chests | A chest that attacks | Chest that is a creature | Later |
| Boss fog wall | Mist at the arena entrance; crossing it commits you | Crossing the archway raises the portcullis behind you (prop already exists) | MVP |
| Classes at the start | Knight, mage, etc. change starting stats | 3 starting Seeds instead of classes: **Fire (strength), Ice (defence), Lightning (speed)** | Later |

## Enshrouded — survival RPG

| Element | How it works there | Adaptation | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Shroud (deadly fog) | Lethal mist with a timer; you can only stay inside for a while | **Ash Mist**: areas where corruption eats away at HP over time; high-level Seeds let you stay longer | Later |
| Flame Altar | Base heart that grows, clears the fog and raises the base level | The **Sanctuary bonfire grows** as you deliver Seeds: unlocks new islands and features | MVP |
| Rescuing NPCs to the base | Found NPCs become blacksmith, alchemist, farmer | NPCs rescued in the ruins (blacksmith, alchemist, explorer) live in the Sanctuary | Later |
| Crafting from materials | Ore, wood, monster parts become gear | Simple: **bones, iron and Ash** from enemies forge sword/armour upgrades | Later |
| Base building | Build anything with voxels | **Not for this game** (huge scope, not souls) | Out |
| Glider and grappling hook | Moves vertically through the world | **Dragon wings** (dragon part) as a glide/dash between platforms | Later |
| Skill tree | Points into branches (warrior, mage, ranger) | Each Seed has a **small tree with 6 nodes** | Later |
| Co-op for up to 16 | Online with friends | **Not for this game** (anti-pillar: no multiplayer) | Out |
| Day/night cycle | Changes lighting and danger | Night: stronger enemies, more Embers | Later |

## Scenery ideas (both games)

- Graveyard with a fallen-knight statue (tutorial).
- Ruined castle wall with a dragon flying in the background (Dark Souls 3's High Wall).
- Sanctuary with a giant bonfire under a burnt dead tree (the "heart" that grows).
- Ash-mist forest with corrupted trees (Shroud).
- Underground forge with lava (blacksmith).
- Bridge over a chasm where the dragon passes and breathes fire (scripted event).

## Suggested order for the next prototype iteration

1. Fallen Guardian (2-phase tutorial boss) in the graveyard.
2. Sanctuary hub: level up with Embers + Sap Shards (flask upgrades).
3. Essence bar for the Fire Seed.
4. Gate that closes behind the player (boss fog wall) + shortcut back to the bonfire.
