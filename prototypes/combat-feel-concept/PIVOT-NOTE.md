# PIVOT NOTE — from combat-feel-concept (2026-10-21)

**Original hypothesis:** dodge-through-telegraph-and-punish souls-like combat is satisfying on touch. REFUTED.

**Keep:**
- The fantasy: rare elemental Seeds of power and growing into a dragon (Dragon Seed).
- Readable enemy attacks (telegraphs) — but short, not the whole loop.
- Touch layout: joystick left, buttons right. Android export pipeline.

**Change (most important):** the core loop — from a slow 1-v-1 duel to **explore and farm**:
roam a small island, fight many monsters fast, gain XP and levels, pick up drops constantly,
and use flashy Seed skills.

**Also change:** visuals — use ready-made CC0 art instead of plain shapes; more on screen happening.

**Revised hypothesis for the next prototype:** if the player roams an island killing monsters
with fast attacks and Seed skills while XP, levels and drops pour in, they will want to keep
playing — true if the user plays 5+ minutes by choice and says they wanted "more".
