# Prototype: Souls 3D (Seeds of the Dragon)

**Hypothesis:** a dark 3D souls-like fight (stamina, roll, lock-on, Seed spell) is fun on a phone.
**Status:** in progress.

## Run

- Desktop: `godot --path .` (WASD move, J attack — hold for heavy, U shield, Space roll, K fire, H flask, L lock-on, N level up at the bonfire, Q switch weapon, right mouse drag camera).
- Android: export the `Android` preset to an APK.
- Mixamo animations are not in the repo (licence); put the Sword and Shield and Great Sword pack FBX files (great sword files prefixed `gs_`) in `assets/mixamo/` named in snake_case.

## Mechanics (v3)

- Sword and shield: 5-hit combo, then an endless chain while your swings keep landing (a different move each press, half stamina; a miss ends it), each hit a different animation; hold ATTACK for a heavy; attacking while sprinting or right after a roll uses dedicated moves.
- Shield block (stamina), parry in the first 0.22 s of a block, shield + attack = kick that breaks guard.
- Poise: enemies get stunned when their poise bar empties; attack a stunned enemy for a critical riposte.
- Sprint (full stick for 0.6 s), lock-on strafing, Fire Seed costs Essence (blue bar).
- Bonfire: rest, level up with Embers; Sap Shards raise max flasks; Embers dropped on death can be recovered.
- Enemy AI: warrior (blocks, then shield-bashes), minion (fast, dodges your swings) and caster (keeps its distance, throws slow-homing ghost fire). They rise from the floor, shamble when far, circle you at mid range, chain a second swing, taunt when you die, and half of the fallen warriors/minions pull themselves back together once after 4 s unless a Fire Seed burns the bones. All clips come from the KayKit skeleton files (the KayKit Knight set from animated-asset-skill is the same rig and clip set, kept in `assets/skeletons/` as reference).
- Sword and shield attacks, Fire Seed cast, flask and parry are hand-made clips (`hero_anims.gd`): key poses with two-bone IK for the hands, blade direction and torso/leg angles, baked into the animation library at load. Walk, run, roll, hits and deaths stay Mixamo. `tools/anim_sheet.gd` renders a clip's key poses, `tools/anim_reel.gd` plays them all for a review video.
- Great sword (ARMA button, Q): two-handed moveset with a 4-hit combo, a spinning heavy, a sliding sprint attack, a jump attack after a roll and its own riposte; more damage, reach and poise damage, costs more stamina and blocking drains more.
- Hero: dark-knight plate armour built in Blender by `tools/make_hero_armor.py` (one GLB, pieces named `<bone>__<piece>` in each Mixamo bone's local space; `hero_look.gd` merges them into one mesh skinned rigidly to the skeleton, one surface per material, to save ~50 draw calls), over the X Bot body as a dark gambeson. Textures in `hero_look.gd` (object-space triplanar) come from Poly Haven CC0 `rusty_metal_04`, `fabric_leather_02` and `rough_linen`, recoloured. `tools/hero_portrait.gd` renders review portraits.
- World (`level.gd`): first loop of the official map (`design/levels/mapa-oficial.md`): Santuário da Chama (hub, y 0) → great stair → Burgo dos Esquecidos (y 15) → Telhados Quebrados parkour (jump and ledge-grab up to y 30, the Becos alley below) → Igreja do Juramento (y 30, Sentinelas de Pedra, bell tower with the 1st Bell) → elevator shortcut back down to the shrine (it starts at the top; riding it opens the shortcut). Falling off the world kills; a crenellated rim keeps combos from carrying you off the shrine. Architecture is modelled, not boxes: `tools/make_architecture.py` builds walls with buttresses and arrow slits, crenellated parapets, lancet-windowed church walls, timber-framed houses with tiled roofs and chimneys, flat-roofed watch houses, quoined ruin towers, iron-strapped beams, stair steps, the open belfry with its spire and rough rock cliffs (`assets/arch`). `level.gd` stretches them to the collision boxes and draws each kind through a few MultiMeshes (48 m cells), textured with the world-space triplanar level materials. The old island (`world.gd`, `terrain.gdshader`) is no longer built.
- Movement: PULAR (F) jumps; in the air, moving into a ledge up to 2.4 m above your feet grabs and climbs it. AGIR (E) uses ladders, bells and elevator calls.
- Breakable pots, jars and barrels (attack, roll or sprint into them): a few Embers, sometimes a flask charge; they come back when you rest at the bonfire.
- Scenery: Gobkit CC0 nature kit (`assets/nature`: pines, cliffs, rocks) outside the walls and Polygonal Mind CC0 ruins (`assets/pm`, see `SOURCES.md`): arch, columns, broken walls, Seed crystals, webs, cart.
- Enemies are our own models (`tools/make_foes.py`, `assets/foes`): Esquecido (hooded hollow villager), Cavaleiro Oco (rusted plate, great helm, red tabard), Clérigo da Cinza (robe, cowl and bone mask, cold eyes; the caster), Espreitador (leather, hood and scarf; dual daggers, or a crossbow with straight fast bolts) and Sentinela de Pedra (stone knight with a horned crest and a stone greatsword). Each part is bound rigidly to one bone of the KayKit skeleton rig so every KayKit clip still plays; face colours are baked into vertex colours, so a foe is one draw call. Weapons are separate GLBs in the KayKit hand-slot frame. The KayKit files in `assets/skeletons` are only the rig source and are left out of the export. Ash Wolves (Quaternius CC0, `assets/beasts`) hunt in packs: run, circle and pounce; the pounce is procedural since the model only has idle/walk.
- Seeds of power: Fire (fireball), Ice (slows for 3 s, heavy poise damage; found in the cemetery) and Lightning (instant strike that jumps to two more enemies; deep in the Dead Forest). SEED casts the current one, TROCAR (T) cycles.
- Area names on entry, fast travel between lit bonfires (VIAJAR / Y).
- Loot: enemies drop items (35%, wolves 25%; bosses a guaranteed Legendary) in 4 rarities (Comum 70%, Raro 22%, Épico 7%, Lendário 1%) with a light beam in the rarity colour. Slots: sword and great sword (+% melee damage), ring (+max HP), amulet (+% Seed damage). Better items equip themselves; worse ones melt into Embers. Equipped gear is listed under the bars.
- RPG systems (`rpg.gd`, decisions in `design/quick-specs/sistemas-rpg.md`): starting class (Cavaleiro, Andarilho, Piromante), 5 attributes bought one point per level at a bonfire, a 3-branch talent tree (1 point every 2 levels, +1 per bell and boss), free-then-paid respec, elements and weaknesses (physical, fire, ice, lightning), status effects (burn, slow, bleed, poison), weapon reinforcement +1..+5 with titanite, quest log with the current objective on screen, and an options page (shadows, 3D resolution, FPS cap, camera sensitivity). `menu.gd` is the pause menu (MENU button or Esc/Tab). `tests/rpg_test.gd` checks the rules headless.
- Look: bright and colourful, no stone or brick textures. Level blocks and modelled pieces use `paint.gdshader` (one clean colour with soft patches, grass or moss on top faces), Kenney kits keep their own colours through `kit_color.gdshader`, the ground is painted per biome (`terrain.gdshader`, rock on steep faces from the face normal). Blue noon, orange dusk, deep-blue night (the day lasts ~60% of the 30-minute cycle), thin sky-coloured haze so far hills stay visible, Filmic tone mapping with extra saturation. MegaKit houses are plaster in six colours (cream, yellow, pink, blue, mint, lavender), one per house.
- Open world (`world.gd`, `hex.gdshader`): built from the KayKit Medieval Hexagon Pack (CC0, `assets/hex`) at giant scale: 32 m hexes on 3 m terrace steps (climb a step: the ledge grab reaches 3.3 m; or walk up the kit's ramp tiles, placed where a hex sits one step below a neighbour), 20 x 20 km from one seed. Biomes (Campos Floridos, Floresta Verde, Bosque Outonal, Pinheiral Nevado, Deserto Dourado, lake shores) repaint the kit's grass and leaves; lakes are water hexes you wade through; high ground grows mountains. 96 towns: castle cities with a ring of walls and gates, villages with fences and a windmill, buildings in one of four kingdom colours (blue, red, yellow, green), joined by road tiles fitted to their open edges (`ROAD_TILES`, measured from the meshes). Each 128 m chunk merges all its hexes into one mesh per biome (a few draw calls) with one concave collision shape (trees do not block). Chests (Quaternius Fantasy Props, animated lid) in the wild and by every keep. A coarse terraced horizon mesh to ~4 km and a castle on the skyline for each town keep the world open; the hand-built valley keeps its own smooth ground inside 360 m.
- Your hero (`hero_body.gd`, `retarget.gd`, APARÊNCIA tab): Quaternius modular outfits on the Universal Base Characters rig: body type, torso, arms, legs and feet (peasant or ranger), pauldron, hood, six hairstyles, beard, and colours for clothes, skin and hair; better pieces and colours unlock with level. A new game opens the creator. The tuned sword moves (Mixamo mocap and the hand-made clips) are retargeted from the X Bot onto your body at load (model-space rotation transfer, hips scaled by height) and cached in `user://`.
- Hero animations: idle, combat idle, walk, run, sprint, jump start / fall / landing, crouch and crouch walk (C / AGACHAR: enemies notice you at under half the distance), ledge climb, swim and swim idle (lakes), roll, strafe, combo, heavy, special moves, spell cast, block, hit, death, getting up at the bonfire, pickup, interact, opening chests, talking (in dialogues), rummaging the bag (menu), cheering a level gained, the bonfire teleport (flash and arrival), and emotes (G / EMOTE: dance, cheer, wave, no, sit, push-ups). Universal Animation Library 1 and 2 clips play on the same rig.
- Enemies and NPCs: the living foes walk a patrol beat until they notice you and stagger when their poise breaks; the dragon bosses chain bites into combos from phase 2, roar at the phase change and summon three servants, and go berserk under 20% HP. Townsfolk work (woodcutter, harvester, waterer, repairer), sit and tell stories, carry loads, drift through idle stances and talk back with gestures.
- City enemies (`tools/make_foes.py`, data in `rpg.gd` FOES): Guarda da Muralha (halberd), Besteiro da Muralha (on the towers), Carrasco (great axe), Feiticeira da Torre (caster), Andarilho da Peste (poisons) and the Cavaleiro Negro guarding each keep. Every foe names its AI (warrior, minion, caster, rogue, archer, sentinel, wolf).
- NPCs (`npcs.gd`, `dialog.gd`): Guardiã Maela at the shrine, Ferreiro Brann (opens weapon reinforcement), Mercadora Ysa (titanite, one Sap Shard) and Velho Corvo in the valley, plus two townsfolk per town. They idle, stroll near home and turn to face you. FALAR opens a dialogue with quick questions and a box to ask anything: Claude answers in character (persona + world lore + your class, level, objective and place) through the Messages API with the player's own Anthropic API key (MENU > OPÇÕES, stored only on the device; Opus or Haiku). Without a key or a connection they use scripted lines.
- Quaternius foes (`qrig.gd`, CC0 Universal Base Characters + Universal Animation Library 1 and 2): Salteador, Atiradora de Facas, Possuídos (zombie walk), Diabrete and Puglin. The library clips are retargeted onto each model at load and aliased to the KayKit clip names, so the same AI drives both rigs. Enemies alert each other within 12 m, no more than two strike at once (the rest circle), keep apart, and back off when badly hurt.
- Hero moves: Mixamo mocap sword slashes and attacks for the 6-hit combo and the heavy.
- Day and night: a 20-minute cycle moves the moon/sun light and fades sky, ambient and fog colours; the HUD shows the hour. AgX tone mapping, glow and slight contrast.
- Save: `user://sementes_save.json`, written on bonfire rest, level up, item/Seed/shard pickup, boss kill, death, Ember recovery and when the app is paused or closed; loaded at start (back at the last bonfire). The autoplay robot neither loads nor saves.
- Title card (CONTINUAR when a save exists, NOVO JOGO wipes it) over the paused world; app icon `icon.png` made from the hero portrait.
- Bosses: Guardian Dragon in the ruins (phase 2 at 50% HP, portcullis closes behind you; drops the Dragon Seed) and the Ancient Wyrm on the summit of Dragon's Peak (bigger, red, 2400 HP, adds a fire rain with ground warning rings; +5000 Embers and +50 max HP). Bosses share one AI; the nearest living one is the target for every other system.
